How this metric works

Skill demand is the share of reviewed signals in the current selection whose posting text matches a skill from a fixed 40-skill taxonomy. The selection uses the published methodology and all-signal denominator.

How to read it

A skill at 22% appears in 22 of every 100 reviewed postings in the selection. Higher means more employers are asking for it. Selections under 100 signals are flagged and should be read with care.

Coverage

May 2026 baseline: 1,304 unique reviewed signals across 6 GCC countries and 20 named cities, collected from 8 to 29 May 2026 from licensed job-search APIs. 37% of signals carry no detectable skill due to sparse retained text and are excluded from skill percentages. Salary appears in only 6.6% of signals, so no salary benchmark is published.
