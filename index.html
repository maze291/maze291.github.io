What I built

A data pipeline that turns messy job-market samples into dashboard-ready views.

01

Cleaned and normalized data

Built Python and Excel/CSV workflows to standardize sampled analyst-role records into comparable fields.

02

Deduped and classified roles

Grouped duplicate records and categorized analyst roles across data, business, BI, finance, operations, product, and strategy paths.

03

Extracted skill indicators

Tracked recurring skill mentions such as SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, Tableau, forecasting, statistics, and data visualization.

04

Market-read summaries

Produced aggregate outputs across city demand, role demand, skill demand, freshness, and confidence metrics for recurring market briefs.

Method

Directional career intelligence, not a job board.

What it measures

Reviewed market records are cleaned, deduplicated where possible, categorized, and converted into aggregate dashboard views.

What it avoids

Public outputs are aggregate-only; raw data and sources stay private by design.

How to read it

Counts represent reviewed roles in this dataset, not a complete count of every open job in the Gulf. Patterns are strongest when they repeat across market reads.
